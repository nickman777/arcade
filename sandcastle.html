How to play

An 8-letter word sits on the beach. Each wave, tap the ONE letter the tide takes, then rearrange what's left into a real word - every tile, no extras. Make it down to a 3-letter word to win the day. Pick the wrong letter to lose and the sand runs out of words. Words are checked live against Wiktionary. ★★★ finish with no resets.
