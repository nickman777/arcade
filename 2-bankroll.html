How to play

Every day one big word sits in the vault, broken into letter chips. Cash out real words (3+ letters) - each chip can only be spent once, across your whole day. Empty the tray for a Clean Sweep. Do it in par words or fewer for the Jackpot. Words are checked live against Wiktionary - a real dictionary, no canned wordlists. The vault word itself doesn't count. ★ bank 80%+ · ★★ clean sweep · ★★★ sweep at par.
