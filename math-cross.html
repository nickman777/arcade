How to play

Every row and every column is a true equation (like 11 × 9 = 99). The yellow numbers and the operators are given - the five dashed squares are the answers. Tap a tile at the bottom, then tap the dashed square it belongs in. Tap a placed tile to pull it back. Use every tile so all six equations check out. One new grid every day.
