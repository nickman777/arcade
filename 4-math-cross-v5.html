How to play

Every row and every column is a true equation (like 11 × 9 = 99), and the grid is one connected crossword: a number you place in one problem is also part of the problem that crosses it. The amber numbers are given; the white squares are empty - some are answers, some are parts of a problem. Tap a tile at the bottom, then tap the white square it belongs in. Tap a placed tile to pull it back. Use every tile so all six equations check out. One new grid every day.
