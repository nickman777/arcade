How to play

Place exactly one queen in every row, every column, and every colored region. Queens can never touch - not even diagonally. Tap a square to mark an X (a square you've ruled out), tap again to clear it. Double-tap a square to place a queen, double-tap again to remove it. Conflicting queens pulse red. Place them all to win.
