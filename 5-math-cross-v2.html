How to play

Tap the blocks in order to build one long combination, like 8 × 4 − 6 + 3. You must use EVERY block. Multiply before you add or subtract (8 × 4 − 6 + 3 is 29, not 14). When the combination equals one of the targets in the box, that target checks off. Clear all three to win. One new board every day.
